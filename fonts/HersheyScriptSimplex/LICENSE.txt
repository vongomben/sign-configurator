Hershey Script 1-stroke (Script Simplex)

Originally prepared in 2011 and converted to SVG fonts in 2019 by
Windell H. Oskay, Evil Mad Scientist Laboratories.
https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts

Contents adapted from the Hershey fonts. This distribution may be used
for any purpose, commercial or otherwise, providing that:

1. The following acknowledgements are distributed with the font data:
   - The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey
     while working at the U.S. National Bureau of Standards.
   - The format of the font data in the distribution this SVG was
     adapted from was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc.,
     900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.

2. The font data may be converted into any other format except the
   format distributed by the U.S. NTIS, where each point is described
   in eight bytes as "xxx yyy:".
